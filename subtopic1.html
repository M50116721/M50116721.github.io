URLWhat is Sportstech and Sports Technology?
Author(s)Stan Cometta
AbstractSports technology or Sportstech refers to the application of digital tools, equipment, and systems that enhance sport business, fan experience, and overall athletic performance. In 2024 the global sportstech market was valued at $17.8 billion and it is projected to reach $117.9 billion by 2034 as a result of growing fan engagements, expansion of sports analytics, investments in immersive experiences, and increased wearable monitoring. Another component transforming the sports industry is machine learning and artificial intelligence. AI is used to create personalized fan connections, enhance experiences in stadiums, scout talent through data metrics, and more. Nowadays technology has improved sports in many ways. Sportstech has revitalized decision making, event planning, revenue growth, and will continue to in the future.

URLThe Evolving Role of Technology and Analytics in Coaching: Transforming Practices and Enhancing the Impact on the Profession
Author(s)Lawrence W. Judge and Matt Moore
AbstractOver the years coaching has evolved alongside technology from the use of simple tools to complex systems which have all transformed the way athletes are trained. Specifically these new complex systems and technologies have enhanced monitoring and decision making by coaches and team leaders. As technology continues to advance it will become increasingly important to maintain a balance between technological application and humanistic coaching. Technology offers incredible data insights but still cannot substitute for mentorship and interpersonal relationships. Alongside technology humane coaching is still deeply rooted in sports due to the trust, adaptability, and the connection it brings. By combining both coaches can create a dynamic and sustainable approach to athlete development.
