URLThe Impact of Emerging Technology in Sports
Author(s)Ohio University
AbstractTechnology is expanding rapidly and now merging into sports. Sports technology is technology that is designed to improve the performance of athletes and the experience of watching sports as they improve how society interacts with sports. These tools have many benefits like having personalized data to improve athlete performance and improved recruitments because coaches can make more informed decisions about players they select. Sport technology includes tools like wearable devices, injury prevention tools, data analytics, and virtual reality. Sport injury prevention tools can include diagnostic programs that use biometric data alongside high-tech mouthguards and helmets and sensor technology. With the help of these technologies sports will reach a new height.

URLWhat Is Sports Technology? Its Impact on Athlete Performance
Author(s) Dr. Matthew Loux and Bryce Loux
AbstractThis article describes Sports Technology as digital technologies that monitor sport events and athletic performance. The field is complex and includes analyses created by AI and VR. Sport technology influences many areas such as athlete performance, sport events, fan experiences, injury prevention, competition preparation, and sport business. Sport technology has many positives but still leaves unanswered questions and concerns like fairness. Some players might not have access to the technologies that other sport players have and cannot improve their performance at the same rate. In the future the sports industry needs to continue participating in responsible and ethical innovation. Additionally they need to ensure fairness and inclusion around the development of technology across all players and sports.
