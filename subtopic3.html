URLThe Impact of Emerging Technology in Sports
Author(s)Ohio University
AbstractTechnology is expanding rapidly and now merging into sports. Sports technology is technology that is designed to improve the performance of athletes and the experience of watching sports as they improve how society interacts with sports. These tools have many benefits like having personalized data to improve athlete performance and better recruitments because coaches can make more informed decisions about players they select. Sport technology includes tools like wearable devices, injury prevention tools, data analytics, and virtual reality. Sport injury prevention tools can include diagnostic programs that use biometric data alongside high-tech mouthguards and helmets, sensors and auto technology. With the help of these technologies sports will reach a new potential.
