URLSports Analytics: How Different Sports Use Data Analytics
Author(s)Kurtis Pykes
AbstractSports analytics refers to the study of athletic performance and business health to optimize the success of sport organizations and all of its processes. Sports analytics is split into two components which include on-field data analytics and off-field data analytics. On field data analytics involves tracking on field data to improve game strategies, player nutrition, and performance levels of athletes. Off-field data analytics involves monitoring off-field data like tickets sales and engagements to inform decisions on team growth and profitability. Data analytics are very important and have become vital to the growth of the sports field in the last few years. The global sports market was able to grow from $354.96 billion to $496.52 billion from 2021 to 2022. Investing in sport data analytics has also grown and was projected to reach $4.5 billion by 2025. Sport analytics will continue to grow and evolve.

URLStatcast
Author(s)MLB
AbstractThe Statcast provided by the MLB is a tracking technology that collects and analyzes large amounts of baseball data. The Statcast is taking steps that have never been done before and is considered a new step in the evolution of baseball. Statcast tracks and quantifies action on the field such as pitching, hitting, running, and fielding. The numbers collected by Statcast become the basis for stat measurements across seasons. Currently Statcast reports the following measurements; arm strength, extension, exit velocity, launch angle, pitch velocity, pop time, spin rate, barrels, blasts, catch probability, expected batting average, expected weighted on-base average, outs above average, and sprint speed. All of these measurements allow scouts, broadcasters, and fans to view the raw skills of their favorite players.
